Quiet Desk Calendar Helper

Quiet Desk Calendar Helper is a small personal tool that lets the owner's own AI assistant, running on the owner's own computer, work with the owner's Google Calendar. It is not offered to the public and is used only by its owner.

What it does

  • Lists events and shows event details from the owner's primary Google Calendar.
  • Creates or deletes a single event, only after the owner approves each action on screen.

What it does not do

  • It does not invite attendees, create video meetings, or send notifications.
  • It does not change calendar settings or sharing.
  • It does not access Gmail, Google Drive, or any other Google service.
